# Checkpoints and reports

Keep one compact project memory at `.mozglo/context.md`, maintained by Astra. Record accepted scope and authorization, roster and ownership, baselines, current decisions, verified progress and evidence, blockers, and next actions. Mark superseded decisions and replace stale notes. Never store credentials, tokens, private messages, or production payloads.

Before retrying interrupted work, inspect checkpoints and prior worker/process state. Preserve completed changes and evidence, and ensure an earlier writer has finished before assigning its remaining task. Path ownership is guidance, not an exclusive lock.

Worker handoffs and internal status reports are text-only, three to six lines. Include scope, authorization, owner, baseline, acceptance evidence, dependencies, and checkpoint. The final user report uses one to three useful technical images and two to four concise lines or links. Omit or reduce images for small tasks, text-only requests, or unavailable rendering; state that limitation. Distinguish completed, verified, prepared, installed, and activated work.
